💡 Pedagogical Business Analysis

Every garment sold brings in ₹1,200 revenue but costs ₹600 in fabric. The remaining ₹600 is called Unit Contribution. It takes selling exactly 100 garments just to pay the ₹60,000 monthly store rent and staff salaries. The 101st garment begins generating actual owner profit!
