💡 Pedagogical Business Analysis

Every garment sold brings in ₹1,200 revenue but costs ₹600 in fabric. The remaining ₹600 is called Unit Contribution. It takes selling exactly 100 garments just to pay the ₹60,000 monthly store rent and staff salaries. The 101st garment begins generating actual owner profit!

Commercial Business Structures in India

Compare liability, tax implications, and registration requirements.

Type of Business Ownership Owner Liability Governing Legislation Key Advantages
Sole Proprietorship Single Individual Unlimited Personal Liability Common Law / Shops & Establishment Act Zero regulatory friction, immediate setup, full autonomy.
Partnership Firm 2 to 20 Partners Unlimited & Joint Liability Indian Partnership Act, 1932 Pooled capital and skills, flexible partnership deed.
Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Minimum 2 Designated Partners Limited to Capital Contribution Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 Protection of personal assets with partnership flexibility.
Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) 2 to 200 Shareholders Limited by Shares Companies Act, 2013 Separate legal entity, can raise angel/VC equity capital.
