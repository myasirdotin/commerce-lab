1. The Two Rules of Debit and Credit

Compare the British Traditional English approach and the American Modern ALCRE approach.

British Method

Traditional Golden Rules of Accounting

Type of Account Debit Rule Credit Rule
Personal Account
People, firms, banks, debtors, creditors		 Debit the Receiver Credit the Giver
Real Account
Tangible & intangible assets (Cash, Furniture, Land)		 Debit what Comes In Credit what Goes Out
Nominal Account
Expenses, losses, revenues, gains (Rent, Salary, Sales)		 Debit all Expenses & Losses Credit all Incomes & Gains
American Method

Modern ALCRE Classification Rules

Account Class Normal Balance To Increase (+) To Decrease (-)
A - Assets Debit DEBIT CREDIT
L - Liabilities Credit CREDIT DEBIT
C - Capital / Equity Credit CREDIT DEBIT
R - Revenue / Income Credit CREDIT DEBIT
E - Expenses / Losses Debit DEBIT CREDIT
💡 Memory Tip: DEAD CLIC → Debit Expenses, Assets, Drawings | Credit Liabilities, Income, Capital.

2. Essential Financial Ratios Master Sheet

Formulas and standard benchmarks used by bankers, analysts, and CBSE/B.Com examiners.

Category Ratio Name Formula Standard Benchmark Business Meaning
Liquidity Current Ratio Current Assets / Current Liabilities 2 : 1 Tests ability to meet short-term debt obligations within 12 months.
Liquidity Quick (Acid-Test) Ratio (Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaids) / Current Liab 1 : 1 Instant debt repayment capacity excluding slow-to-liquidate stock.
Solvency Debt-to-Equity Ratio Total Long-Term Debt / Shareholders' Equity ≤ 2 : 1 Proportion of outsider leverage funding company vs owner equity.
Profitability Gross Profit Margin (Gross Profit / Net Revenue) × 100 Industry-dependent (>30%) Profit margin left after direct production costs to cover overheads.
Profitability Net Profit Margin (Net Profit after Tax / Net Revenue) × 100 > 10% - 15% Final bottom-line return generated from each rupee of turnover.
Activity Inventory Turnover Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Higher is better (6x - 12x) How many times physical stock is sold and replaced during the year.

3. Excel & Spreadsheet Shortcuts for Accountants

Speed up spreadsheet bookkeeping and financial reporting.

Formatting Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + Shift + 4 ($): Apply Currency Format (₹)
  • Ctrl + Shift + 5 (%): Apply Percentage Format
  • Ctrl + Shift + 3 (#): Apply Date Format (DD-MMM-YY)
  • Ctrl + 1: Open Format Cells Dialog

Navigation & Selection

  • Ctrl + Arrow Keys: Jump to edge of data table
  • Ctrl + Shift + Down: Select entire data column
  • Ctrl + Space: Select entire column
  • Shift + Space: Select entire row

Formulas & Auditing

  • Alt + =: AutoSum instant formula insertion
  • F4: Toggle Absolute Cell Lock ($A$1)
  • Ctrl + ~: Toggle formula view across sheet
  • Shift + F3: Insert Function wizard
