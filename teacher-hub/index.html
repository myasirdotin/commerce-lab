Lesson: Demystifying the Accounting Equation

Objective: Students understand why every transaction maintains Assets = Liabilities + Capital without rote memorization.

Teaching Sequence:
  • 00-15m: Physical balance scale demonstration

Holistic Grading Rubrics

Standard criteria for grading students' double-entry books and spreadsheet projects.

Performance Dimension Exemplary (90-100%) Proficient (75-89%) Developing (50-74%)
Classification Accuracy Flawless distinction between Real, Personal, and Nominal accounts; accurate ALCRE categorizations. Minor confusion on compound entries; standard transactions correctly recorded. Frequent misclassification between expenses and capital assets.
Arithmetical Integrity Trial balance and accounting equation balance automatically without plug figures. Trial balance balanced with minor guidance during ledger posting. Unbalanced trial balance due to debit-credit omission.
Commercial Analysis Articulates clear managerial rationale for gross margin, working capital changes, and tax liabilities. Accurately computes net profit and ratios with basic narrative understanding. Calculates raw figures but unable to interpret whether the business is healthy.
