Commerce in Islam is governed by divine ethics, justice ('Adl), and mutual consent (Taradi). Learn how to conduct trade according to Shariah standards, identify practices that violate Islamic law (such as Riba, Gharar, and Maysir), and implement authentic Halal alternatives.

STRICTLY HARAM

Prohibition of Riba (Interest)

Money cannot breed money without genuine risk-bearing and underlying asset transfer. Conventional bank interest, compounding loans, and late penalties violate Quran 2:275.

STRICTLY HARAM

Prohibition of Gharar & Maysir

Excessive ambiguity, hidden terms, naked short-selling, binary options, and speculative derivative betting without asset ownership are prohibited by the Prophet ﷺ (Muslim 1513).

OBLIGATORY (FARD)

Amanah & Business Zakat

Absolute transparency in weights, measures, and defect disclosure (Muslim 101). Annual payment of 2.5% Zakat on net commercial liquid inventory (AAOIFI FAS 9).
