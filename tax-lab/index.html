1. Input Stage (Purchase)

✓ Saved as Input Tax Credit (Asset)

2. Output Stage (Sale)

Output GST Collected: ₹3,600
CGST (9%): ₹1,800 | SGST (9%): ₹1,800
Liability collected from buyer

3. Net Government Settlement

NET GST PAYABLE (Output - Input Credit)
₹1,800

Because you collected ₹3,600 from your buyer and already paid ₹1,800 to your supplier, you only deposit ₹1,800 with the Government on Form GSTR-3B.

Why does this matter?

Without Input Tax Credit (the cascading tax effect in pre-GST days), the consumer would be taxed on tax, raising prices artificially!
