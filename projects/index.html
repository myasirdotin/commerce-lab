Graded Commercial Projects & Case Studies

Real businesses don't write textbook answers; they maintain books, file GST returns, and make management decisions under uncertainty. Complete these graded portfolio challenges.

🥉 Bronze Project Est. 2 Hours

Retail Kirana Month-1 Bookkeeping

Record 15 foundational transactions for "Gupta Grocery Store", post into T-account ledgers, prepare a balanced Trial Balance, and construct the first month's Trading & P&L Account.

Rubric Criteria:
  • ✓ 100% correct Golden Rule debit/credit entries
  • ✓ Arithmetically balanced Trial Balance
  • ✓ Correct computation of Gross Profit and Net Profit

🥈 Silver Project Est. 4 Hours

Boutique Cafe Unit Economics & BEP

Analyze a Bengaluru artisan coffee shop with ₹1,20,000 monthly rent and salaries. Calculate contribution margins for coffee beans vs pastries, establish the Break-Even Point, and build a stress-test model for monsoon season footfall drops.

Rubric Criteria:
  • ✓ Separation of fixed overheads vs variable inputs
  • ✓ Weighted contribution margin calculation
  • ✓ Margin of Safety sensitivity analysis
Open Business Lab
🥇 Gold Project Est. 6 Hours

E-Commerce MIS & GST Reconciliation

Ingest a 100-row multi-state electronic sales and purchase register in Excel. Use SUMIF, XLOOKUP, and pivot tables to calculate net GST liability (CGST/SGST/IGST), offset Input Tax Credit (ITC), and design an executive KPI dashboard.

Rubric Criteria:
  • ✓ Error-free spreadsheet formula implementation
  • ✓ Statutory compliance with CBIC ITC rules
  • ✓ Executive dashboard presentation with AOV and margin cards
