Structured Curricula

6 Master Learning Pathways

Each pathway follows an active pedagogy: Concept → Real Example → Interactive Exercise → Practice → Quiz.

01

Accounting Foundations

Terminology, the Accounting Equation, classification of assets/liabilities/capital, and the fundamental rules of debit and credit.

Explore Pathway 1 →
02

Financial Accounting

Journal entries, compound journals, T-account posting, Trial Balance balancing, Cash Book, BRS, and depreciation methods.

Explore Pathway 2 →
03

Financial Statements

Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account, and Balance Sheet. Understand Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Profit, and Owner's Equity.

Explore Pathway 3 →
04

Business & Entrepreneurship

Business models, fixed vs. variable costs, break-even unit analysis, pricing strategies, cash flow management, and customer profitability.

Explore Pathway 4 →
05

Taxation Lab (India)

Direct vs. indirect taxes, GST slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%), Input Tax Credit offsetting, TDS rules, and small business presumptive schemes.

Explore Pathway 5 →
06

Excel for Commerce & MIS

Master business formulas (SUMIF, COUNTIF, XLOOKUP, IF) and build executive MIS reporting dashboards with KPIs and aging schedules.

Explore Pathway 6 →
